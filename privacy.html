Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short

  • Without an account, your plan never leaves your device.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address and your study plan so it can sync between your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Countdown Study Planner ("the app", "we") is made by an independent developer based in India. Contact: vansh778855@gmail.com. This address is also our contact for questions and complaints about your personal data.

Using the app without an account

You can use every planning feature without signing in. Your deadline, subjects, chapters, dates, study days, settings and focus-timer minutes are saved only on your device, in its local storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps a copy of your plan in your account so it appears on every device where you sign in. We collect:

  • Email address: to send sign-in links and to identify your account.
  • Google account details (only if you choose "Continue with Google"): Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We use only the email address, to identify your account. We don't access anything else in your Google account.
  • Your study plan: the plan details listed above, so it can sync.
  • Your consent record: the date you agreed to these terms when signing in.
  • Security logs: our sign-in provider records technical details such as IP address, browser and time of sign-in, to protect accounts from abuse.

Why we use it

We use this information only to provide sign-in and sync, to keep accounts secure, and to reply when you contact us. We rely on your consent, which you give when you tick the box before signing in. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and synced plans: until you delete your account. Daily backups are deleted after 30 days, so a deleted account disappears from backups within 30 days.
  • Data on your device: until you choose Start over, sign out and clear it, or uninstall the app.
  • Security logs: kept by our providers for a limited time under their own policies.

Your rights

  • See and copy your data: everything we store is visible in the app, and Settings › More › Copy backup code gives you a full copy.
  • Correct it: edit anything directly in the app.
  • Delete it: see below.
  • Withdraw consent: sign out and delete your account.
  • Complain: email us first and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also contact your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: tap your account icon, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and the plan saved in it.
  • Without the app: email vansh778855@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Plan on this device: Settings › More › Start over erases it, as does uninstalling the app.

Children and young people

Using the app without an account involves no personal data. To sign in, you must be 18 or older, or have permission from your parent or guardian, who agrees to this policy on your behalf. The app is not meant for children under 13.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your plan. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us straight away if you notice anything wrong.

Cookies and device storage

The app doesn't use cookies. It uses your device's storage only for things it needs to work. Details are on our Cookies and storage page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
