Cookies and storage

Effective 28 October 2026

We don't use cookies. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no third-party cookies.

The app does save a few things in your device's own storage, because it can't work without them:

WhatWhyHow long
Your study planSo your deadline, subjects and chapters are there when you come back, even offlineUntil you choose Start over or uninstall
Your settingsRemembers your theme and that you've seen the tipsUntil you clear them or uninstall
Sign-in session (only if you sign in)Keeps you signed in and lets your plan syncUntil you sign out
Offline copy of the website (website only)Lets the website open without an internet connectionUntil your browser clears it

Do you need to accept anything?

No. All of this storage is strictly necessary for features you use, so there's no cookie banner to accept.

How to clear it

  • In the app: Settings › More › Start over erases your plan and settings on this device.
  • If you're signed in: open your account and choose Sign out.
  • In a browser: clear the site's data in your browser settings. On a phone: uninstall the app.

More about how we handle data is in our Privacy Policy.
